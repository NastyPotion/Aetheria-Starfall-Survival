# Agreed survival campaign

The player begins beside the ship with **two filled hearts (4 health points)**, no crafted weapon, and an empty carried inventory. All champions can heal up to six hearts with food. The playable application remains Java 21 with the standard library.

| Level | Enemy kills | Monster kills | Gather this level | Craft and consume | Diamonds secured this level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 easy enemy | 2 monsters | 1 meat, 2 metal, 2 stone | Pickaxe: 2 metal + 2 stone | At least 1 |
| 2 | 2 stronger enemies | 2 monsters | 3 metal, 3 stone | Sword: 3 metal + 3 stone | At least 4 |
| 3 | 3 enemies | 10 fast monsters | 4 metal, 4 stone, 4 rocks | Gun: 4 metal + 4 stone + 4 rocks | At least 10 |

The user explicitly approved these quantities. Meat is food and an achievement, not an ingredient consumed by the pickaxe recipe. Stone and rock are separate inventory resources. Level-three health and stamina are exactly twice the corresponding level-two values at the same difficulty, including rounding. Difficulty changes hostile counts, speed, health and stamina; it never changes recipes or achievement targets.

Loose materials enter the inventory. Trees hide food, scrap metal and stone. Rocks contain rough rock and diamond deposits; a crafted pickaxe is required to mine those. Gathering may happen while exploring, but crafting requires the level's kill and gather achievements and its actual recipe materials. Crafted equipment carries into later levels.

Diamonds in the carried inventory **do not count** toward level completion. The explorer must survive the journey back and press F at the ship to deposit them after completing preparation/crafting. Depositing transfers them from carried cargo to secured progress. Death loses carried resources, including diamonds, while secured diamonds, equipment and completed achievements remain. Respawning begins beside the ship with two hearts.

At each completed level, the player explicitly chooses **Stay and explore** or **Next level**. Staying never advances automatically. Level three offers staying or returning home to complete the mission. The final message is: "Congratulations, you will return back to the planet and help your people. You are so brave."

The menu provides Start Game, champion selection (Astra, Nyx, Orion), difficulty selection (Easy, Medium, Hard), Continue, Save and Quit. The HUD shows carried resources at the top, next steps in the upper middle area, and a ship radar. Inventory and level-specific achievement panels explain progress and recipes. Character names appear below their sprites.

Controls: WASD/arrows move; E/Space attack; Shift dash; F gather/deposit/interact; I inventory; C crafting; J achievements; Q eat meat; 1-4 equip; N choose advancement at the ship; Tab map; H guide; P/Escape pause; F5/F9 save/load.
